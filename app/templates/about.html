About ITS Flow

ITS Flow (IT Service Flow) is a ticket tracking system for a company IT desk.

Staff use it to report an IT problem: they enter their name, department and a description of the issue, and the ticket goes straight to the IT Desk.

The IT Desk sees every ticket in one list and can open any ticket to read the details.

How it works

  1. Choose Staff or IT Desk on the home page.
  2. Staff fill in the ticket form and see "Your ticket has been sent to the IT Desk".
  3. The IT Desk opens the dashboard to see all tickets and their status.
